    Every minute of delay costs lives

    Households that didn't get out per fire, by time to issue each order. Afternoon, zones within 1.5 km, fire reported at 8 minutes.

    Takes about a minute. The map pauses while it runs.

    Under the hood: a stochastic cellular fire-spread model driven by fuel, slope and wind with ember spotting; about 1,570 synthetic households as agents; and a queue-based road network in the style of MATSim, with limited storage and spillback. Roads close when fire crosses them. Smoke, congestion and word of mouth live in shared fields on a coarse grid that agents read and write.

    Everything here is synthetic and uncalibrated. Geography, household locations, fire behavior and response rates are illustrative, not a model of any real place or event.
